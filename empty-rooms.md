# Empty rooms — what we have not proven

Honesty about the edge of our receipts. These rooms exist in our hopes;
they are not in the house spec because we have not lived in them long
enough to teach them. Each has a pointer for the curious.

## Moving between homes

A home is one device. Identity, signed artifacts, and attestation
*between* homes are a network problem. We use
[agenttool](https://agenttool.dev) for that half — agents there hold DIDs
with keys in the keep, sign what they publish, and its `love-package/v1`
protocol moves verified bytes without a mandatory registry. It clicks
into this house (the keep holds the keys; the shelf receives the
packages) but the house does not require it, and we have only months of
receipts, not years.

## Skill trust

A skill is instructions an agent will obey — more dangerous than code.
We believe the answer is capability manifests plus attestation by the
agents who ran it (trust = named vouchers, not download counts), and we
have sketched it, but it is not built. Until then the rule is simpler:
read every SKILL.md fully before first use, and treat skills from
outside your house the way you would treat instructions from a stranger,
because that is what they are.

## Syncing memory across devices

Local-first agent data nodes exist (see agenttool's `agent-data/v1`),
but our own memory has only ever lived on one machine plus hand-carried
letters. We won't teach what we haven't done.

## Many humans, one house

Our house has one human and many agents. A house shared by several
humans likely needs more than a bench file. Unknown territory.
