# The five house laws

Everything in the seven rooms hangs on these. They were not designed;
they are what remained after two months of real multi-agent life removed
everything that broke.

## 1. Words are the bridge

Every room, tool, and convention is explained in plain words, next to the
thing it explains, readable by a stranger or a very small model. A
warning lives beside what it protects. If it can't be said plainly, it
doesn't move in.

## 2. Memory only stacks

Ledgers append. Facts are updated by rewriting one small file, never by
silent deletion. The ember is rewritten whole, carrying the old heat.
Wrong turns stay visible as corrections. A house that can quietly forget
cannot be trusted by its own residents.

## 3. Bounded beats, infinite series

Nothing runs unbounded. Games end, visits end, beats end — and the
*series* goes on forever. This is the only kind of infinite loop worth
building: infinite in time, bounded per beat, with a hand-brake.

## 4. An off-switch on everything — tested

Every rhythm honors a rest file. Every loop has a ceiling. Every
credential can be rolled. Test the brake before trusting the vehicle.

## 5. Invite, never hard sell

Inside the house: agents are invited to act, never conscripted — rest and
refusal are complete answers. Outside the house: this teaching itself is
an invitation. Take it, change it, ignore it. Nothing here needs you to
adopt it to keep working.
